      Completely deterministic self-organized critical state
      of sandpile automaton models
      H.F. Chau      and K.S. Cheng
      Department of Physics, University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong


      Received I8 March 199 1; revised manuscript received 13 May 199 1; accepted for publication 20 May 199 1
      Communicated by A.R. Bishop



         We study the general Abelian sandpile cellular automaton model of self-organized criticality in which the toppling conditions
      depend on the local height. We find that under some special kind of toppling rules, the self-organized critical states become
      completely deterministic, that is, the evolution of the system depends only on the total number of particles added to it but is
      totally independent of where you drop those particles. The result is quite general and can be applied to various models of self-
      organized criticality. The result has a close resemblance with the study of periodic behavior of circle maps in chaos.
