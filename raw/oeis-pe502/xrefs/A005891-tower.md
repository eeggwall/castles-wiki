## Proof

The A160747 proof (`A160747-tower.md`) with t = 4: a castle of width 3 with column heights
c(i) >= 1 has c(1) + Sum_{i=2..3} max(0, c(i) - c(i-1)) blocks, and c(i) - 1 are the bead
counts of a 4-core with one corner fewer (Cho et al., Theorem 3.3). On p. 14 the paper states
cc(4, m) = (5m^2 + 5m + 2)/2, the entry's name. The g.f. numerator 1 + 3x + x^2 is
(N(3,1), N(3,2), N(3,3)); the semiperimeter of a castle is 3 + (n + 1).
