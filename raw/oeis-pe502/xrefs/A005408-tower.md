## Proof

Proposition 3.4 at `t = 3`: `min(m, 1) = 1` term, `N(m, 1) * binomial(2m + 1, 2m) = 2m + 1`
for `m >= 1`, and the empty partition is the one 3-core with 0 corners. The paper states it on
p. 14: "Clearly, cc(2, m) = 1, cc(3, m) = 2m + 1" (text layer
`raw/cho-huh-nam-sohn-2022-bounded-free-motzkin-paths.txt` L803).
