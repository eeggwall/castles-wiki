## Proof

The A160747 proof (`A160747-tower.md`) with t = 3; on p. 14 the paper states cc(3, m) = 2m + 1.
Directly: a castle of width 2 has c(1) + max(0, c(2) - c(1)) = max(c(1), c(2)) blocks, and
(n+1)^2 - n^2 = 2n + 1 pairs of heights >= 1 have maximum n + 1.
