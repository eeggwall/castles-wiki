## The facts (verified)

A271942 = "Triangle read by rows: T(n,k) is the number of bargraphs of semiperimeter n having
width k (n>=2, k>=1)" (Emeric Deutsch, May 21 2016), offset 2, keyword `tabl` (row `n` is
`k = 1..n-1`), 91 terms. Its Cf. is `A082582, A145904, A271943`. It cites none of the entries
below, and they cite it:

- A001263's approved formula (A001263 #792): `Sum_{k=1..n} T(n,k)*binomial(m+n-k-1, n-1) =
  A271942(n+m, n)` for `n, m >= 1`, and `Cf. A005408, A005891, A063490, A160747, A271942.`
- A063490, A160747 and A005891 (approved): `a(n) = A271942(n+4, 4)`,
  `a(n) = A271942(n+6, 5)` and `a(n) = A271942(n+4, 3)`.

A bargraph of width `k` with column heights `c_1..c_k` has semiperimeter
`k + c_1 + Sum_{i>=2} max(0, c_i - c_{i-1})`, the width plus the number of blocks of the
castle with those heights, so column `k` counts the castles of width `k` by blocks.

1. **Columns k = 2..5 are A005408(n-3), A005891(n-4), A063490(n-4), A160747(n-6)**, for all
   listed terms of the four entries (66, 47, 41 and 34), counting the bargraphs directly.
2. **A001263's formula** gives all 91 listed terms of A271942.
3. **Column `k` is row `k-1` of A145904.** A145904 is "Square array read by antidiagonals:
   Hilbert transform of the Narayana numbers A001263", and A145905 defines the Hilbert
   transform of a triangle `L` as the square array whose row `n >= 0` has g.f.
   `R(n,x)/(1-x)^(n+1)`, `R(n,x)` the row polynomial of `L`. Row `k-1` of A145904 is then
   `N_k(x)/(1-x)^k` with `N_k(x) = Sum_{j=1..k} A001263(k,j)*x^(j-1)`, whose coefficient of
   `x^(m-1)` is `Sum_{j=1..k} A001263(k,j)*binomial(m+k-j-1,k-1)`, which is `T(m+k,k)` by
   A001263's formula. So `T(n,k) = A145904(k-1, n-k-1)` (row, column), and the rows of
   A271942 read backwards are the antidiagonals of A145904: all 11175 b-file terms, and the
   55 data terms of A145904.
4. **`T(n,k) = A273350(n,n-k)`.** A273350 counts bargraphs of semiperimeter `n` by up steps;
   the up steps of a bargraph add up to `n - k`, since its semiperimeter is its width plus its
   total height of up steps. Checked against A273350's g.f. to `n = 60` and its 66 data terms.
   With 3, A145904 and A273350 are the same array, which both entries conjecture ("This seems
   to be a duplicate", Alois P. Heinz, Jun 04 2016). Neither A271942 nor A273350 cites the
   other.

Verified 2026-11-01 against the live data; script below. `bin/python/tower_t_cores.py` checks
the four columns and A001263's formula against all 11175 terms of the A271942 b-file.
