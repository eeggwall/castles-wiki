## Identity (verified, note the offset shift)

A063490 = "a(n) = (2n-1)(7n^2-7n+6)/6", **offset 1**, data `1, 10, 40, 105, 219, ...`.

    a(n) = T(4, n-1) = number of towers of width 4 with exactly n-1 blocks.

(So a(1) counts towers with 0 blocks, a(2) with 1 block, etc.  This is the only tier-2
entry with offset 1 — the others are offset 0.)

## The tower object (self-contained definition)

A **tower** of width w is a stack of unit-height blocks on a line of w cells: each block
is a horizontal segment of adjacent cells at some height, every block rests on the floor or
on the block directly below it (nothing floats or overhangs), and two blocks in the same
row are separated by at least one empty cell.  Equivalently a tower is a column-height
vector (c_1,...,c_w) of nonnegative integers; its number of blocks is the number of
maximal horizontal runs, c_1 + Sum_{i=2..w} max(0, c_i - c_{i-1}).

## Draft additions

### Comment (append)

> a(n) is also the number of towers of width 4 with n-1 blocks, where a tower is a stack
> of unit-height blocks on a line of cells such that every block rests on the floor or on
> the block below, and two blocks in the same row are separated by at least one empty cell.
> Equivalently, it is the number of quadruples of nonnegative integers (c1,c2,c3,c4) with
> c1 + max(0,c2-c1) + max(0,c3-c2) + max(0,c4-c3) = n-1.

### Formula (append)

> a(n) = Sum_{k=1..4} N(4,k) * C(n + 3 - k, 3), where (N(4,1),...,N(4,4)) =
> (1, 6, 6, 1) are the Narayana numbers (A001263); equivalently the generating function is
> x*(1 + 6x + 6x^2 + x^3)/(1-x)^4.
>
> Sanity check (n is the 1-based index): a(1) = C(3,3) = 1 (0-block tower), a(2) =
> C(4,3)+6*C(3,3) = 4+6 = 10 (1-block tower).
