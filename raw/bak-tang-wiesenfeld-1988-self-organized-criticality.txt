                                                     Self-organized criticality

                                              Per Bak, Chao Tang, and Kurt Wiesenfeld
                            Department   of Physics, Brookhaven National Laboratory, Upton, New York 11973
                                                        (Received 28 August 1987)
                     We show that certain extended dissipative dynamical systems naturally evolve into a critical state,
                  with no characteristic time or length scales. The temporal "fingerprint" of the self-organized criti-
                  cal state is the presence of flicker noise or 1/f noise; its spatial signature is the emergence of scale-
                  invariant (fractal) structure.
