                 We study a general Bak-Tang-Wiesenfeld-type        automaton model of self-organized criticality in which
              the toppling conditions depend on local height, but not on its gradient. We characterize the critical
              state, and determine its entropy for an arbitrary finite lattice in any dimension. The two-point correla-
              tion function is shown to satisfy a linear equation. The spectrum of relaxation times describing the ap-
              proach to the critical state is also determined exactly.
