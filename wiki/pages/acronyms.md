# Acronyms and abbreviations

The abbreviations used on the castles wiki, in alphabetical order. Each is also spelled out at its first use on the page that uses it.

- **AC** - *Analytic Combinatorics* (Flajolet and Sedgewick, Cambridge University Press, 2009).
- **AM** - abelian model: Dhar's name for the general sandpile with an integer toppling matrix `Δ` (1990).
- **AOCP** - *The Art of Computer Programming* (Knuth); the same as TAOCP.
- **ASM** - abelian sandpile model, the name later papers, Chau and Cheng's among them, use for Dhar's abelian model.
- **BDD** - binary decision diagram.
- **BM** - the Berlekamp-Massey algorithm.
- **BSGS** - baby-step giant-step, a discrete-logarithm search.
- **BTW** - Bak, Tang and Wiesenfeld, and their sandpile model of self-organized criticality.
- **CCC** - convex castle count, the Representations subpage's name for the number `A_convex(w, h) = C(2h + w − 3, w − 1)` of convex castles of width `w` and height `h`.
- **CD** - compact disc.
- **CEILIDH** - the torus-based public-key cryptosystem of Rubin and Silverberg (2003), on `T_6`.
- **CEV**, **COD** - the convex castles of area `n` with an even and with an odd number of blocks, written `cev(n)` and `cod(n)`; `cev(n) + cod(n) = A001523(n)`.
- **CF** - continued fraction.
- **CRT** - Chinese remainder theorem.
- **CYC** - cycle, a construction of the symbolic method.
- **DCT** - discrete cosine transform.
- **DFA** - deterministic finite automaton.
- **DFS** - depth-first search.
- **DFT** - discrete Fourier transform.
- **DLP** - discrete logarithm problem.
- **DOI** - Digital Object Identifier.
- **DP** - dynamic program.
- **DTMF** - dual-tone multi-frequency, the telephone keypad tones.
- **DVD** - digital versatile disc.
- **e.g.** - for example (Latin *exempli gratia*).
- **EGF** - exponential generating function.
- **EIS** - *Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences*; *Analytic Combinatorics* cites OEIS entries as "EIS A…".
- **FFT** - fast Fourier transform.
- **FLAC** - Free Lossless Audio Codec.
- **FS** - forcibly simple, of a tournament score vector.
- **FSC** - forbidden subconfiguration, in Dhar's test for recurrent sandpile configurations.
- **GF** - generating function.
- **GNU** - GNU's Not Unix, the name of a free-software project.
- **HTTP** - Hypertext Transfer Protocol.
- **i.e.** - that is (Latin *id est*).
- **ISBN** - International Standard Book Number.
- **JIS** - *Journal of Integer Sequences*.
- **JPA** - Jacobi-Perron algorithm, a multidimensional continued fraction.
- **JPEG** - Joint Photographic Experts Group, and its image format.
- **KMS** - Kac-Murdock-Szegő, of the symmetric Toeplitz matrix `ρ^|a−b|`.
- **KP** - key pulse, the start signal of an MF code.
- **LFSR** - linear feedback shift register.
- **LGV** - Lindström-Gessel-Viennot, of the lemma on non-intersecting lattice paths.
- **LUC** - the public-key cryptosystem of Smith and Skinner (1995), based on Lucas sequences.
- **MF** - multi-frequency, the telephone network's signaling tones between exchanges.
- **MFM** - modified frequency modulation, a recording code of floppy disk drives.
- **MNT** - Miyaji, Nakabayashi and Takano, of the elliptic curves of their 2001 paper.
- **MSET** - multiset, a construction of the symbolic method.
- **NTT** - number-theoretic transform.
- **OCR** - optical character recognition.
- **ODE** - ordinary differential equation.
- **OEIS** - On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences.
- **OGF** - ordinary generating function.
- **PDF** - Portable Document Format.
- **PE** - Project Euler; "PE 502" is Project Euler problem 502, the castle-counting problem.
- **PNG** - Portable Network Graphics.
- **PSET** - powerset, a construction of the symbolic method.
- **RLL** - run-length limited, of a constrained binary sequence.
- **RNA** - ribonucleic acid; the RNA secondary structures counted by A004148 are its planar pairings.
- **SCC** - strongly connected component.
- **SEQ** - sequence, a construction of the symbolic method.
- **SOC** - self-organized criticality, or self-organized critical.
- **TAOCP** - *The Art of Computer Programming* (Knuth); the same as AOCP.
- **URL** - Uniform Resource Locator.
- **XTR** - the subgroup-trace public-key cryptosystem of Lenstra and Verheul (2000).
- **ZDD** - zero-suppressed binary decision diagram.
